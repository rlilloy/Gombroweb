A free alternative to Scrivener

Scrivener is a very complete program, and for many projects it's still the best choice. Gombro offers something else: working paragraph by paragraph, doubting, branching and deciding later. And it costs nothing.

What Gombro is

Gombro is a creative writing editor for novelists, built in Argentina by Raül Lilloy. It runs on your computer, with no cloud and no artificial intelligence autocompleting your text.

Each project is organized into chapters, sessions and paragraphs. Gombro has no character or location sheets: you follow them with tags, hashtags, collections and the graph.

When Scrivener suits you better

  • Academic or research-heavy work, with PDFs and documents inside the project.
  • Scripts, or e-books generated from the same program.
  • Also writing on iPhone or iPad.
  • Needing a spell checker: Gombro doesn't have one.

When Gombro suits you better

  • You write novels and want to keep your doubts open until you decide: variants live together on the page.
  • You want to follow characters and motifs across the book with the graph.
  • You use Linux, or would rather not pay for a license.

How to bring your novel over from Scrivener

Gombro doesn't open .scriv files: the novel comes over compiled.

  1. In Scrivener, compile the manuscript to Word (.docx).
  2. Use Heading 1 for chapters, and a *** separator or Heading 2 between scenes.
  3. In Gombro, click ↑ in the explorer and choose the file. Each Heading 1 becomes a chapter, and each scene a session.

Anything outside the compiled manuscript, such as the research folder, isn't imported.
